Privacy Policy

Creator Tools ("the tool") is operated by an individual creator for publishing videos to their own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the account owner.

Data we access

  • Through TikTok Login Kit: the basic profile of the authorized account (open ID, display name, avatar) — used only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok for that account.
  • Videos and captions we created ourselves and choose to upload.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer in a file readable only by the owner. They are never sold, shared or sent to any third party other than TikTok's API.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this site.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; the local token file is then deleted.
