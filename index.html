Creator Tools

A private publishing tool for our own video channels

Creator Tools is a small, private desktop tool used only by the owner of the channels GTA 6 Daily Clips and AmedsporHaber. It takes short vertical videos that we edit ourselves and uploads them to our own TikTok accounts — as drafts for review, or as posts — through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes our own accounts.
  2. When a new video is ready, the tool uploads it to the same account (scope video.upload; video.publish after approval).
  3. The owner reviews and publishes in the TikTok app. No other users' accounts or data are accessed.
